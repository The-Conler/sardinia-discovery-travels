Raw elegance, pristine shores

Rugged, wild, and undeniably beautiful, Corsica and Sardinia offer an escape into the untouched heart of the Mediterranean. Corsica stuns with its dramatic mountain landscapes, hidden beaches, and rich French-Italian culture, while Sardinia’s Costa Smeralda is famed for its turquoise waters, luxury resorts, and vibrant nightlife. Whether you seek adventure or relaxation, these islands offer a perfect mix of nature, history, and indulgence.

Catamaran

A wide, stable deck with room to spread out in the sun or the shade. The day runs at an easy pace, with stops in sheltered bays for swimming and snorkelling. Departures from Santa Teresa, Stintino, Olbia, Palau, Alghero and Cagliari.

Sailboat

Engine off, sails up. A quieter way to follow the coast, moving with the wind and dropping anchor where the water is clearest. Departures from Santa Teresa, Porto Torres, Golfo Aranci, Arbatax, Alghero and Cagliari.

RIB

Fast, light and close to the water. A small group, a nimble boat and the freedom to reach coves and caves that larger boats pass by. Areas include La Maddalena, Alghero, Cala Gonone and Cagliari.

Yacht
